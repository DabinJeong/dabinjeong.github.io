## Personal interest

Outside of research, I'm into 🧶 knitting and 🎲 ♟️ board games and chess. 💬 Linguistics is the one I keep coming back to. I'm fascinated by the subtle gap between symbols and the concepts they represent, as René Magritte was: how symbols come to embody concepts, how concepts reshape symbols, and how this relationship evolves. I owe a lot to my heroes, Douglas Hofstadter and Terry Winograd. [Gödel, Escher, Bach], [Surfaces and Essences], and [Language as a Cognitive Process] are my favorite books. Happy to chat about this!
